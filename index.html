THE PROBLEM

When seeing isn't
always believing.

AI has made digital content easier to create, edit, and imitate. As the line between authentic and synthetic media becomes harder to recognize, users need better ways to evaluate what they encounter online.

01

Looks real.

AI-generated images and manipulated media can appear authentic at first glance.

02

Spreads fast.

One uncertain post can travel through feeds, chats, and communities in seconds.

03

Verify first.

TrustNova adds an AI-powered assessment to support a more informed choice.
